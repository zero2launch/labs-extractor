Google Labs Token Extractor - Privacy Policy

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data to external servers.

Data Handling:
- Session tokens are extracted from Google Labs pages only when the user clicks the extension icon
- Tokens are stored locally in the browser's storage
- No data is sent to third-party servers
- No analytics or tracking is performed
