AI Session Token Extractor - Privacy Policy

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal user data to external servers. All data remains on your local device.

Data Handling:
- Session tokens are extracted automatically from labs.google and sora.chatgpt.com pages when they load.
- Tokens are stored locally in the browser's chrome.storage.local area.
- No data is ever sent to third-party servers.
- No analytics or tracking is performed.
